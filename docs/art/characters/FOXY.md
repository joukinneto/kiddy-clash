# Foxy — Production Character Sheet

Workflow: `KIDDY-ART-001`

Owners: `KC-CHARACTERS` + `KC-ART-DIRECTOR`

## Identity

Species: orange fox.

Role: smart, quick, playful and inventive.

Ability: **Dash Rápido / Quick Dash**.

## Canonical silhouette

- pointed fox ears
- large fluffy tail with light tip
- slim/compact runner body
- goggles clearly visible
- confident diagonal/action poses

## Face

- orange fur
- cream/white muzzle and cheek fur
- large expressive eyes
- playful eyebrow language
- friendly grin
- occasional wink is acceptable in presentation art

## Outfit

- blue adventure/sport top
- goggles on forehead
- dark gloves/shoes
- small utility/adventure accents

## Palette direction

- fur: saturated orange
- muzzle/tail tip: cream/white
- outfit: vivid blue
- goggles: dark strap with green/teal lenses
- dark glove/boot accents

## Motion personality

Foxy should feel clever and nimble rather than simply fast.

### Idle

- tail motion
- playful weight shift
- goggles catch light

### Run

- low agile posture
- tail counterbalances turns
- quick foot cadence

### Dash

- orange/blue directional streak
- brief anticipation
- strong forward silhouette
- clear start/end state
- no attack/contact damage

### Jump/fall

- tail exaggerates balance
- ears react to vertical movement

### Finish / celebration

- confident pointing/wink or quick spin
- playful, not arrogant

## Gameplay readability

Orange fur must contrast with warm environment props using:

- blue outfit mass
- cream face/tail tip
- dark accents

## Prohibited drift

Do not:

- remove goggles from default identity
- reduce tail until fox silhouette is lost
- make Dash a weapon/attack
- copy an existing franchise fox character
