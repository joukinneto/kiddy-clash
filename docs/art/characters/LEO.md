# Leo — Production Character Sheet

Workflow: `KIDDY-ART-001`

Owners: `KC-CHARACTERS` + `KC-ART-DIRECTOR`

## Identity

Leo is the primary Kiddy Clash hero and the visual anchor of the franchise.

Species: young lion.

Role: brave, energetic, friendly leader.

Ability: **Super Salto / Super Jump**.

## Canonical silhouette

- large rounded mane creating a clear circular head silhouette
- compact athletic body
- oversized paws and shoes
- short limbs
- visible tail tuft
- strong forward-running pose
- crown motif readable even at small sizes

## Face

- warm golden/orange fur
- large expressive brown eyes
- rounded muzzle
- small dark nose
- broad friendly smile
- eyebrows capable of clear anticipation/excitement
- never intimidating or aggressive

## Outfit

Base identity:

- red hero top/hoodie
- blue shorts/pants accents
- blue wrist/arm accents
- red/blue athletic shoes
- gold crown emblem

Cosmetics may vary but the crown motif and red/blue hero identity must remain recognizable.

## Palette direction

- fur: warm gold/orange
- mane: deep warm brown
- hero red: saturated warm red
- hero blue: medium/deep royal blue
- crown: bright gold/yellow
- muzzle/highlights: warm cream

## Motion personality

Leo moves with confidence and bounce.

### Idle

- subtle breathing
- mane/tail secondary motion
- occasional confident smile/blink
- weight centered and ready

### Run

- forward lean
- exaggerated paw/foot arcs
- strong arm swing
- mane/tail follow-through
- readable cadence on mobile

### Jump

- quick anticipation
- legs compress
- arms open slightly
- cheerful upward expression

### Fall

- legs/arms widen for balance
- expression stays playful, not fearful

### Super Jump

Must feel clearly stronger than normal jump:

1. squash/anticipation
2. gold crown/star flash
3. powerful launch
4. warm yellow trail
5. strong apex silhouette
6. landing dust + brief squash

### Finish / celebration

- raised fist or open victory arms
- crown/star motif
- joyful smile

## Gameplay readability

Leo must remain readable against:

- blue sky
- green grass
- warm brown platforms

His red shirt and dark mane should create reliable contrast.

## Prohibited drift

Do not:

- make Leo realistic/scary
- reduce the mane until he reads like another animal
- change the base species
- remove the crown identity from the default hero
- turn Super Jump into an attack
- give weapons
