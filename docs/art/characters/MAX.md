# Max — Production Character Sheet

Workflow: `KIDDY-ART-001`

Owners: `KC-CHARACTERS` + `KC-ART-DIRECTOR`

## Identity

Species: brown/white puppy.

Role: curious explorer, loyal friend and treasure seeker.

Ability: **Detector de Tesouros / Treasure Finder**.

## Canonical silhouette

- floppy ears
- explorer hat
- backpack
- compact puppy body
- compass/adventure accessory
- one paw often leading/pointing

## Face

- brown/white fur pattern
- large warm brown eyes
- dark rounded nose
- broad friendly smile
- curious eyebrows

## Outfit

- tan explorer hat with paw badge
- explorer vest/shirt
- backpack
- red neck scarf
- compass/adventure accessory

## Palette direction

- fur: warm caramel/brown + cream white
- explorer tan: khaki/sand
- scarf: warm red
- backpack: olive/brown
- compass accents: brass/gold + teal/green

## Motion personality

Max should feel curious and determined.

### Idle

- looks around
- sniffs or checks compass
- ears bounce gently

### Run

- backpack secondary motion
- ears flap slightly
- enthusiastic paw/foot cycle

### Treasure Finder

- compass pulse
- subtle gold/teal scan arc
- nearby treasure/star cue
- information/reveal ability only
- no combat effect

### Jump/fall

- backpack and ears follow through
- expression stays adventurous

### Finish / celebration

- raises compass or points toward next adventure

## Gameplay readability

Brown fur and tan gear must not blend into wooden bridges/platforms.

Use:

- red scarf
- cream face
- green/teal compass accents
- controlled outline/shadow separation

## Prohibited drift

Do not:

- remove explorer hat/backpack from default identity
- make detector reveal personal/player information
- use realistic military/tactical styling
- turn the ability into an attack
