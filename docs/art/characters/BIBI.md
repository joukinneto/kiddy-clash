# Bibi — Production Character Sheet

Workflow: `KIDDY-ART-001`

Owners: `KC-CHARACTERS` + `KC-ART-DIRECTOR`

## Identity

Species: white bunny.

Role: cheerful, agile, competitive and energetic.

Ability: **Corrida Turbo / Turbo Run**.

## Canonical silhouette

- tall rabbit ears
- compact athletic body
- large feet/shoes
- visible fluffy tail
- energetic forward-leaning pose
- pink headband/sport identity

## Face

- white fur
- large dark expressive eyes
- pink nose
- happy open smile
- upbeat competitive eyebrows
- friendly, never smug or hostile

## Outfit

- pink athletic top
- dark/neutral shorts
- pink/white shoes
- pink wrist/head accents
- bunny icon may appear as a secondary motif

## Palette direction

- fur: clean warm white
- accent pink: saturated candy/sport pink
- secondary pink: light blush
- shorts: charcoal/dark neutral
- highlights: white

## Motion personality

Bibi is the quickest-looking hero.

### Idle

- light bouncing
- ears move independently
- ready stance

### Run

- rapid cadence
- ears stream backward
- long foot arcs
- subtle pink speed streaks at higher speed

### Jump/fall

- ears exaggerate vertical motion
- feet remain readable

### Turbo Run

- pink/white speed trail
- compact forward pose
- short acceleration squash
- no teleporting
- no collision damage

### Finish / celebration

- energetic hop
- ears upright
- arms raised

## Gameplay readability

Bibi's white/pink palette must remain distinct from sky/clouds through:

- dark shorts
- pink outlines/accents
- shadow/rim treatment

## Prohibited drift

Do not:

- shorten ears enough to lose rabbit silhouette
- remove pink athletic identity from default form
- turn Turbo Run into an attack
- use overly realistic rabbit anatomy
